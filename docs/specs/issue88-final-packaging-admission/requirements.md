# Requirements: authentic FINAL packaging admission

## 功能概述
The existing publisher authenticates the selected FINAL thirteen-job metadata alongside full integration. Both observations remain partial evidence inside a globally blocked publisher; synthetic cases do not establish actual FINAL/native acceptance.

## 历史经验与范围边界
Reuse the fixed TLS reader, strict FINAL snapshot, existing bounded pagination and exact historical byte adapters. Preserve the integration-only wrapper and canonical digest. Complete legacy fixtures before mutation so integration negative cases cannot pass for a missing FINAL endpoint. Archive/native byte ownership and every other release gate remain separate.

## 需求列表
### FR-1 Fixed FINAL identity (Must)
WHEN admission observes FINAL THEN it SHALL authenticate the selected repository, source commit/tree, candidate branch ref, fixed active workflow ID/path/blob, newest selected run/current attempt1 and thirteen exact successful job IDs/names. FINAL SHALL require push from refs/heads/release/full-rc-candidate-*; integration MAY use its independently supported branch or lightweight-tag ref at the same source.
### FR-2 Repeated complete observations (Must)
WHEN either selected workflow has newer, incomplete, failed, skipped, denied, changing or truncated evidence THEN admission SHALL fail closed without older-green fallback. Both complete evidence passes and closing newest/current/source observations SHALL agree.
### FR-3 Exact frozen digests (Must)
WHEN observations agree THEN canonical integration and FINAL metadata SHALL match their already selected gate digests. The integration schema and integration-only request sequence SHALL remain unchanged. FINAL SHALL use rc-final-packaging-v1 with its own invocation and source/workflow observation.
### FR-4 One bounded operation (Must)
WHEN aggregate admission starts THEN it SHALL validate and clamp one absolute deadline to at most300 seconds and preserve cancellation across every helper and both gates. Expiry/cancellation SHALL produce no partial report or deadline reset.
### FR-5 Remaining gates block publication (Must)
WHEN these two gates authenticate THEN the private immutable report SHALL mark only full_integration/final_packaging passed, all other rows unknown, status blocked, approvals false and draft visibility unknown. Actual entry and every mutation sink SHALL stop before staging or writes, retaining blocked_no_effect and no transition. Caller-report evaluate SHALL remain unauthenticated.
### FR-6 Exact historical composition (Must)
WHEN source admission runs THEN only D[M], I[M,D], J[R,I] with exact four R documents SHALL qualify. All nine replacement inverses SHALL recover complete immutable M bytes. Selected/historical content failure SHALL be terminal; only initial topology mismatch MAY delegate. Original pins and actual candidate bytes SHALL not be normalized or weakened.
### FR-7 Complete source-bound regression (Must)
WHEN validating THEN old1268/strict303 unique IDs SHALL remain and new24 TLS plus12 composition cases SHALL run without skips or exceptional successes. Intended legacy assertion extensions SHALL preserve IDs and ensure mutated integration endpoints are reached. Existing read-only workflow SHALL run all120 cases with exact source checks and unchanged permissions/events.
